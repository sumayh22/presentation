Coffee… A Journey of Flavor

From a tiny bean in the African highlands to a cup that brings people together everywhere.

A Brief History

A Story That Began in East Africa

Legend says the shepherd Kaldi noticed his goats becoming unusually energetic after eating the berries of a strange shrub.

Coffee spread to Yemen and the Arab world, then reached Europe, becoming one of the most enjoyed drinks on earth.

Farm to Cup

A Long Journey Before the First Sip

  • Growing and caring for coffee trees.
  • Picking ripe cherries and extracting the seeds.
  • Roasting, grinding, and brewing.

Bean Varieties

Every Bean Has Its Own Character

  • Arabica: rich flavors and gentle acidity.
  • Robusta: a bold body and higher caffeine.
  • Liberica: a rare bean with a fruity character.

Brewing Methods

The Method Shapes the Cup

  • Paper filter: a clean, delicate flavor.
  • French press: a full body and natural oils.
  • Espresso: intense and topped with dense crema.

Quality & Excellence

What Is Specialty Coffee?

Coffee cared for from farm to cup, scoring 80 points or above under recognized quality standards.

It is often roasted lighter to reveal its original flavors and unique story.
